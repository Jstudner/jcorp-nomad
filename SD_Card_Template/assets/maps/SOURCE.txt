Vendored for the Maps page (maps.html):

- maplibre-gl 6.11.2 (BSD-3-Clause, LICENSE-maplibre.txt): maplibre-gl.mjs imports
  ./maplibre-gl-shared.mjs and starts ./maplibre-gl-worker.mjs relative to its own URL,
  so the three files stay together.
- pmtiles 4.5.0 (BSD-3-Clause, LICENSE-pmtiles.txt): reads .pmtiles by byte range.
- @protomaps/basemaps 5.7.2 (BSD-3-Clause, LICENSE-basemaps.md): builds the map style
  (light, dark, white, grayscale, black) for Protomaps v4 tiles in the browser.
- protomaps/basemaps-assets 028c18f: fonts/ (Noto Sans Regular, Medium, Italic; Latin,
  Greek, Cyrillic, Armenian, Hebrew and punctuation ranges; SIL OFL, fonts/OFL.txt) and
  sprites/v4/ (MIT, sprites/LICENSE.md).
