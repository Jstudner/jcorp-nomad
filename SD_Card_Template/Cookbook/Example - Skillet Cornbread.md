Crisp-edged cornbread baked in a skillet that goes into the oven empty and hot.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/4 cups buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- 6 tbsp butter
- salt to taste

## Steps
1. Put the skillet in the oven and heat both to 425°F.
2. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, salt and baking powder together.
3. Beat the buttermilk and eggs, then stir them into the dry mix until just combined — a few lumps are fine.
4. Melt the butter in the hot skillet, swirl to coat, and pour the rest into the batter.
5. Pour the batter into the skillet. It should hiss.
6. Bake 20-25 minutes, until the top springs back and the edges have pulled away.
7. Turn it out onto a board and cut it in the skillet's shape.
