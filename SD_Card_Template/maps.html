Chrome on Android

Once per phone:

  1. Copy this, paste it into a new Chrome tab and open it:
    chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure
  2. In the box, put Nomad's address:
    http://192.168.4.1
  3. Set it to Enabled, tap Relaunch, then tap the location button again
